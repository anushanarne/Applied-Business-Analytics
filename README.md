Repository containing solutions, code, and case studies for Applied Business Analytics—covering linear algebra, statistics, hypothesis testing, and machine learning concepts.
# Applied Business Analytics - Capstone & Solutions

Welcome to the **Applied Business Analytics** repository! This repository contains code, theoretical explanations, calculations, and case studies covering key quantitative and analytical concepts essential for data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📌 Topics Covered

### 1. Linear Algebra & Vectors
* **Vectors & Scalars:** Definitions, geometric and algebraic properties, vector magnitudes, and directions.
* **Vector Operations:** Addition, subtraction, scalar multiplication, and dot products.
* **Matrices & Transformations:** Square vs. rectangular matrices, basis, linear transformations, and eigenvectors.

### 2. Calculus & Machine Learning
* **Derivatives & Partial Derivatives:** Concepts and applications in optimization.
* **Gradients & Optimization:** Role of gradients, gradient descent, and backpropagation in training algorithms.

### 3. Probability Theory
* **Fundamentals:** Primary components of probability, conditional probability, and Bayes' Theorem.
* **Random Variables & Distributions:** Discrete vs. continuous distributions, expected values, and central tendencies.
* **Core Laws:** Law of Large Numbers (LLN) and Central Limit Theorem (CLT).

### 4. Data Summarization & Joint Probability
* **Outlier Detection & Treatment:** Interquartile Range (IQR), Z-scores, trimming, and winsorization.
* **Joint Probability Distributions:** Joint vs. marginal probabilities, covariance, independence tests, and correlation coefficients.

### 5. Statistical Inference & Hypothesis Testing
* **Sampling Methods:** Random, stratified, cluster, and systematic sampling.
* **Parameter Estimation & Testing:** Null vs. alternative hypotheses, p-values, one-tailed vs. two-tailed tests.
* **Statistical Tests:** t-test, Chi-Square test, and Analysis of Variance (ANOVA).
* **Experimental Design & A/B Testing:** Designing controlled experiments and evaluating marketing/business interventions.

### 6. Applied Problem-Solving & Case Studies
* Worked numerical solutions covering z-scores, probability scenarios (e.g., Poisson/Binomial time-interval probabilities), confidence intervals, and margin of error calculations.
